Why Diffie-Hellman Matters

Proposed in 1976 by Whitfield Diffie and Martin Hellman, this groundbreaking protocol was the first practical method allowing two remote parties to securely exchange cryptographic keys over an unencrypted network.

Try Secret Messaging →

  • Powers TLS 1.3 & HTTPS

    Ephemeral Diffie-Hellman (DHE / ECDHE) secures billions of web browsing sessions every single second across the globe.

  • Guarantees Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS)

    Even if long-term server private keys leak in the future, past recorded sessions remain impossible to decrypt.

  • Secures Signal, WhatsApp & SSH

    Enables end-to-end encrypted messaging apps and remote server shells to negotiate symmetric session keys effortlessly.
